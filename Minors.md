# 1♣ and 1♦

**Law:** SYSTEM.md §2 (1♣ / 1♦), §4, §5  
**Status:** Stub · 26_08_27

**3–3 minors → 1♣. 4–4 minors → 1♦.** Neither is forcing. Partner may pass 1♣. Unlimited. 1♣ is **not** a strong club and **not** alertable.

---

## How high

Opener may be a min **or** the 15–18 notrump. The **first** bid assumes a min. After `1m – 1M – 1NT` you know 15–18.

**First response:**

| | HCP | Bid |
|--|-----|-----|
| **Quit** | 0–5 | Pass (no major). 6–10 no 4M: **1NT**. Light 1M with 6+ and 4 cards is OK. |
| **Invite** | 11–12 | 2NT (no 4M) or inverted 2m (minor-oriented). |
| **Force / game** | 13+ | 2/1 in a new minor, or 3NT. |

**After 1m – 1M – 1NT (15–18):**

| | HCP | Bid |
|--|-----|-----|
| **Quit** | **6–7** | **Pass.** |
| **Invite** | **8–9** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> (2-way NMF). |
| **Force / game** | **10+** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♦</span> GF, or 3NT. |

A courtesy 1♥ on 6 does not become 3NT when they rebid 1NT.

## <span style="color:#C62828">Inverted raises</span>

| Call | Meaning | Table |
|------|---------|--------|
| <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> over 1♣ | Limit+ clubs | **Alert** |
| <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♦</span> over 1♦ | Limit+ diamonds | **Alert** |
| 3♣ / 3♦ | Weak / mixed raise | Card |

**2/1 in a new minor is GF** (1♣–2♦, 1♦–2♣). **Walsh** over 1♣: bid a four-card major; 1♦ denies one (or diamonds and can stand 1NT from opener).

## The 1NT rebid (15 to a bad 18)

Gavin’s 2-way NMF is written opposite a *weak* 1NT rebid. Ours is the **strong** one. Same gadget, swapped bucket.

| Opener | Meaning | Table |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 1NT | **15 to a bad 18**, balanced-ish | Explain if asked — **not** an announcement |
| Jump 2NT | ~18–19 | Natural |

A 12–14 balanced hand already opened 1NT. It is not here.

### <span style="color:#C62828">Two-way NMF</span>

After `1m – 1M – 1NT`:

| Call | Meaning | Table |
|------|---------|--------|
| <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> | Puppet to 2♦; then invite or weak diamonds | **Alert** |
| <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♦</span> | Artificial GF checkback | **Alert** |
| 2M | To play | — |

**Kantar is off.**

### Example — strong rebid

**Dealer South · neither vul**

| | ♠ | ♥ | ♦ | ♣ |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| **S** | AJx | Kxx | AQxx | Kxx |
| **N** | Kxxx | Qxxx | xx | xxx |

| West | North | East | South |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| | | | 1♦ |
| Pass | 1♥ | Pass | 1NT |
| Pass | 3NT | Pass | Pass |
| Pass | | | |

**Why:** South is 17 balanced — too good for the opening 1NT. North has game opposite 15–18.

**Tell the opponents:** If they ask about the 1NT *rebid*: “fifteen to eighteen, balanced.” Do not announce it.
