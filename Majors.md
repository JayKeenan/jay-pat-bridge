# 1♥ and 1♠

**Law:** SYSTEM.md §2 (1M) and §6  
**Status:** Version 00 · 26_08_27

Five-card majors, unlimited. A 12–13 balanced usually opened **1NT**. Reverse **outside** 2/1 = **18+**.

**Stay safe.** Raise with a raise. 8–10 dummy with three trumps is **2M**, even with a five-card other major.

---

## How high (starting points)

Opener is often 12–14 but may be 19 — he will try if he has extras. **8–10 does not bid game.**

| | Strength | Bid |
|--|----------|-----|
| **Quit** | **0–7** | Pass 1M, or 1NT then 2M if they bid. |
| **Constructive** | **8–10** dummy, 3 trumps | **2M**. Not 1♠. Not 4M. |
| **Mixed** | **8–10**, 4 trumps | **3M** |
| **Invite** | **11–12** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">3♦</span> with 4 trumps; 1NT then 3M with 3. |
| **Force / game** | **13+** | 2/1, Jacoby (4+), splinter. Bypass 4♠ over 1♥. |

## Raise with a raise

Constructive ~8–10 dummy and **three** trumps: bid **2M**. Do **not** start **1♠** over 1♥ to show a side five-card major.

North `♠ AT753  ♥ Q85  ♦ K87  ♣ 65` — 8 HCP, three hearts. Dummy ≈ 9.

Jay opens **1♥**. **2♥** is right. **1♠** is the rainbow: after 2♦, 2♥ sounds like a doubleton and the next bid is a game you never had. If you already bid 1♠ and they bid 2♦: **2♥** to play.

**Same hand, majors reversed** (five hearts, three spades, partner opened **1♠**): **2♠**. 2♥ would be 2/1 GF — you already knew not to bid that on 8. 1♠ over 1♥ is *not* GF, but it is still not a raise. **2M** both ways.

## <span style="color:#C62828">3♦ — 4-card limit</span>

**Dealer South · neither vul**

| | ♠ | ♥ | ♦ | ♣ |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| **S** | AKxxx | Qx | Kxx | xxx |
| **N** | Qxxx | Axx | Axxx | xx |

| West | North | East | South |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| | | | 1♠ |
| Pass | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">3♦</span> | Pass | 4♠ |
| Pass | Pass | Pass | |

**Why:** Four trumps, limit. 2♠ would have been the 8–10 three-card raise; 3♠ mixed (four trumps, less than a limit).

**Tell the opponents:** Alert 3♦ — “four-card limit raise.”

## <span style="color:#1565C0">1NT — semi-forcing</span>

**Announce** “semi-forcing.” Opener passes only with a hand that would **not** accept a limit raise.

**15 to a bad 18 5332 does not bid 2NT.** 2NT is **18–20**. That hand rebids **2♣ or 2♦**, often a **three-card** minor, and catches up later.

**Hearts only:** `1♥ – 1♠ – 1NT` = 15 to a bad 18, 5/6 hearts. After 1♠ there is no such step.

### Example — three-card minor, extras later

**Dealer South · neither vul**

| | ♠ | ♥ | ♦ | ♣ |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| **S** | AQx | AJxxx | Kxx | Kx |
| **N** | xxx | xx | Qxxx | Axxx |

| West | North | East | South |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| | | | 1♥ |
| Pass | <span style="color:#1565C0;font-weight:700">1NT</span> | Pass | 2♦ |
| Pass | Pass | Pass | |

**Why:** South is 16, 5332, too good to pass 1NT, too small for 2NT (18–20). 2♦ is three diamonds.

**Tell the opponents:** Announce 1NT “semi-forcing.” 2♦ is natural (may be three).

## Two-over-one

**2♦** and **2♥** promise **five**. When you need a GF and do not have that suit, **<span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span>** (alert) is the catch-all.

| Auction | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| 1♠ – 2♥ | GF, **5+** hearts |
| 1♥ – 2♦ / 1♠ – 2♦ | GF, **5+** diamonds |
| 1♥ – 2♣ / 1♠ – 2♣ | GF, **alert** — clubs, **or** no five-card red 2/1 |

**Over 1♥, four spades and a GF: skip 1♠.** Start 2♣ or 2♦ (if 5♦). **1♥ – 1♠** then a later force shows **five** spades.

| Opener after 2/1 | Meaning |
|------------------|---------|
| Rebid of the major | **6+** |
| Reverse or 3-level | **Shape, not extras.** A minimum may do it. |
| 2NT | Not six of the major |

Reverse = 18+ still applies **outside** 2/1.

**Tell the opponents:** Alert 1M–2♣ — “game force, not necessarily clubs.” 2♦ / 2♥ are 2/1 GF, five-card, on the card.

## Passed hand — Reverse Drury

| Auction | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Pass – 1M – <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> | Limit-type raise. **Alert.** |
| Opener’s 2♦ | Minimum |
| Opener’s 2M or higher | Extra / accept |

Unpassed 2♣ is the GF catch-all, not Drury.
