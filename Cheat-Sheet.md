# Cheat sheet — three 2♣ gadgets

**Law:** SYSTEM.md (Drury §6, 2-way NMF §5, Crawling Stayman §3)  
**Status:** Version 00 · 26_08_28  
Cover the answers. Name the call. Then check.

The trap is always **2♣**. It is not one bid. It is three.

| Auction | 2♣ means | Alert / announce |
|---------|----------|------------------|
| You **passed**, they open **1♥ / 1♠** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">Reverse Drury</span> — limit raise | **Alert** “Drury” |
| They open **1NT** | Crawling Stayman — you have a **4-card major** | Card. Crawls **alert** |
| `1m – 1M – 1NT` or `1♥ – 1♠ – 1NT` | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2-way NMF</span> — **invite** puppet | **Alert** “2♣ invite, 2♦ GF” |
| You have **not** passed, they open **1♥ / 1♠** | GF catch-all | **Alert** “GF, not necessarily clubs” |

Unpassed 1M–2♣ is **never** Drury. Invite with **no** 4-card major after 1NT is **never** Stayman — that is <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♠</span>.

---

## 1. Reverse Drury

You **passed**. Partner opens **1M** in third or fourth. You have a limit raise (~10–11, 3+ trumps).

You: <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span>.

| They bid | Meaning | You |
|----------|---------|-----|
| **2♦** | Minimum | Pass 2♦, or **2M** to play. Game is off. |
| **2M** or higher | Extras | We are going. |

A courtesy 8-count still raises **2M**. Drury is the invite you could not make at your first turn.

---

## 2. Two-way NMF (opposite 15–17)

They opened a suit and rebid **1NT**. That is **15–17**. You already showed a major.

| You mean | Bid |
|----------|-----|
| **6–7** | **Pass**, or **2M** to play (five of your major) |
| **8–9** invite | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> → they **must** bid 2♦ → then 2M / 2NT / 3m, or **pass 2♦** with weak diamonds |
| **10+** force | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♦</span>, or **3NT** |

After `1♥ – 1♠ – 1NT`: **2♥** = preference, to play. **2♠** = to play (5+). **2♣ / 2♦** still the gadget.

---

## 3. Crawling Stayman

They opened <span style="color:#1565C0;font-weight:700">1NT</span>. You have a **four-card major**. (No four-card major + invite = <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♠</span>, not 2♣.)

They: **2♦** = no 4-card major; **2♥** = 4♥; **2♠** = 4♠, not 4♥.

**Weak:** if they bid your major, **pass**. If **2♦**, do not pass 2♦ —

| You bid | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **2♥** | Weak hearts, **or** both majors (pass or correct) |
| **2♠** | Weak spades only |

If they bid **2♥** and you wanted spades: **2♠**. Alert the crawls.

**Invite** with a 4-card major: 2♣, then 2NT (no fit) or raise.

Auction: Pass – Pass – 1♥ – Pass – ?

**2.** Same cards, but you did **not** pass. Partner opened **1♥** in first seat. ?

**3.** Partner opened 1♥ in third. You passed, Drury 2♣. They bid **2♦**. ?

**4.** `♠ K10xxx  ♥ Qxx  ♦ xx  ♣ Qxx` (8, five spades).  
Auction: 1♦ – Pass – 1♠ – Pass – 1NT – Pass – ?

**5.** Same auction. You hold `♠ Kxxx  ♥ Qxx  ♦ xx  ♣ xxxx` (6, four spades). ?

**6.** Same auction. You hold `♠ AKxxx  ♥ Kxx  ♦ xx  ♣ Qxx` (13, five spades). ?

**7.** Partner opened <span style="color:#1565C0;font-weight:700">1NT</span>. You hold `♠ Qxxx  ♥ Jxxx  ♦ xx  ♣ xxx` (4). They bid 2♦ over your 2♣. ?

**8.** Partner opened 1NT. You hold `♠ Axx  ♥ Kxx  ♦ Qxx  ♣ Jxxx` (11, no 4-card major). ?

**9.** Partner opened 1NT. You hold `♠ KQxx  ♥ xx  ♦ xxx  ♣ xxxx` (5, four spades). They bid **2♥** over your 2♣. ?

**10.** `1♥ – 1♠ – 1NT`. You hold `♠ Kxxxx  ♥ Qx  ♦ xxx  ♣ xxx` (6, five spades). Want out of NT. ?

---

## Answers

| # | Bid | Why |
|---|-----|-----|
| **1** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> | Passed-hand limit raise. Drury. Not 2♥ (that is 8–10). Not 3♥. |
| **2** | **3♥** | Mixed: 8–10, **four** hearts, unpassed. **2♣** would be GF. That is the Drury trap. |
| **3** | **2♥** | They showed a min. Sign off. Do not bid game. |
| **4** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> then **2♠** | Invite, five spades. Opposite 15–17. |
| **5** | **Pass** | 6–7 opposite 15–17. Stay. |
| **6** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♦</span> or **3NT** | 10+. Force. |
| **7** | **2♥** | Crawl. Both majors, weak. Pass-or-correct. Do **not** pass 2♦. |
| **8** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♠</span> | Invite, **no** 4-card major. Not Stayman. |
| **9** | **2♠** | Crawl to spades. They showed hearts; you did not come for hearts. Alert. |
| **10** | **2♠** | To play. Not 2♣ (that is the invite gadget). |
