## Do not alert

- 1♣ (three-plus clubs).
- 2/1 new suits (on the card as GF).
- Weak twos, including 2♦.
- Natural game tries, natural reverses.

If unsure: **alert** and tell the truth. A slow natural explanation beats a missed red card.
