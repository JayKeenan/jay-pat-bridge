# Doubles — precis

**Not law.** Working picture: **Gavin.** Ceilings later. Longer paper: [Doubles](Doubles.md). Takeout of their opening: `COMPETITIVE.md` §2.

A double is a **word**. The auction picks which word.

Matchpoints. Stay safe. Plus scores.

---

## The four that do almost all the work

| Word | Who you are | They did | It means |
|------|-------------|----------|----------|
| **Takeout** | We have not bid a suit | Opened a **suit** | Short theirs. **All** unbid suits. Partner **bids**. |
| **Negative** | We opened; you have not named a suit | **Overcalled** | The **other major** (four). Five: *bid* it. |
| **Responsive** | Partner doubled or overcalled | **Raised** | The **other major**. Not a stack in their suit. |
| **Support** | We opened; partner bid **1♥/1♠** | They bid | **Three** of partner’s major. Raise = **four**. |

**Penalty** is the leftover: we already have a fit, we bid NT, they are high, or you **pass** partner’s takeout double with a stack (that pass *converts*).

One leak: responsive is **not** “I have hearts after they raised hearts.” That hand **passes**.

---

## Takeout — `(1♦) X`

Short diamonds, can stand the unbid suits, especially the majors. Opening values with shape, or a tank that will bid again. Four-card majors **double**; a real five-card major **overcalls** (unless too strong → X then bid the suit). Partner is supposed to take it out.

---

## Negative — `1♦ (1♠) X`

You are responder. Four **hearts**, enough to compete. `1♣ (1♥) X` = **exactly four spades**; `1♠` is five. `1♣ (1♦) X` = both majors. After `1♥ (1♠)` there is no unbid major — leftover **minors**, the exception. A stack in their suit: **pass** (hope opener reopens).

---

## Responsive — `(1♥) X (2♥) X`

Partner already entered. They raised. Your double is **spades**, not hearts. Raise partner if you have a raise. If they bid a *new* suit, this word is off.

---

## Support — `1♣ – 1♠ – (2♦) X`

Opener: **three** spades. `2♠` = four. Usual ceiling through **2♠**. Same idea as a support **redouble** if they doubled 1M. This is about the **fit**, not takeout of their suit.

---

## Support cue (not a double, same sitting)

In competition, jump raise is **preempt**; mixed 3M is off. Cue of *their* suit = **limit+ raise**. Simple raise = compete.

---

## Penalty

We want to defend. Low-level, almost never the first meaning. Matchpoints: +200 is a top; −590 is a zero.

---

## The rest of the dictionary

Exist; we have not adopted them as a set.

| Name | Typical meaning |
|------|-----------------|
| **Reopening / balancing** | Same *word* as takeout, later in the auction, often a king lighter. Opener reopens with X so partner can pass for penalty. |
| **Lead-directing** | “Lead this.” Common on their artificial bid (Stayman, splinter, transfer). **Lightner** at slam: unusual lead, often dummy’s first suit. |
| **Maximal** | Competitive raise already used; X = **game try**. |
| **Card-showing / action / optional** | Values, not a suit. Partner may pass or bid. Fuzzy; needs a card. |
| **Snapdragon** | Fourth suit: that suit + tolerance for partner’s. Optional gadget. |
| **Rosenkranz** | They doubled partner’s overcall; XX or raise shows/denies a top honor. Optional. |
| **Equal-level conversion** | Takeout X, then a new suit at the *same* level still takeout-ish (minors). Expert option. |
| **Vs 1NT** | Penalty, or a one-suiter (DONT), or values. **Later** for us. |
| **High-level X** | More often penalty or “cards”; takeout only if you agreed it. |

**Redouble** is a different word: after their takeout of our 1M, usually **values** (not a raise). Transfers in that seat are later.

---

## How to pick, at the table

1. Has our side named a suit?
2. Is there an **unbid major**?
3. Did they **raise**, or bid a **new** suit?

Unbid + they opened a suit → takeout.  
We opened, they overcalled, you have no suit yet → negative.  
Partner entered, they raised → responsive.  
Partner bid 1M, they bid → support X.  
Fit or NT already → penalty.

Low doubles are almost never “I have their suit.” Negative and responsive **compete**; they are not invites. The **cue** is the invite.
