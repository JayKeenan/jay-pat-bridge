# What we are doing

**Law:** SYSTEM.md §1  
**Status:** Draft 26_08_27  
No announce. No alert (except in the examples below).

Bidding is not a description of your hand. Vanderbilt did not invent a game of “tell partner about J9 doubleton.” We are trying to **play our highest-scoring contract** and, at the least cost to us, **get in their way** while they try to find theirs.

Every call is a choice among plus scores: 2♥ making vs 4♥ down vs 3NT vs defending. Showing a fifth spade is useful **only if** it gets us a better one of those. If mentioning it means we can no longer play 2♥, it was not information. It was a donation.

No system is perfect. A century of designers have traded one set of misses for another. We will miss some 5–4 other-major partscores on purpose so we can **stop in 2M** on the 8-counts. That is the tradeoff. It is not a failure to describe.

**Before you bid, two questions:**

1. What contract are we trying to play?
2. If I make this call, can we still stop there?

If (2) is no, and we were never in game, pick another call.

Gavin and Kokish assume responder already knows quit / invite / force. This partnership has leaked: opener bids, and responder will not stop below game with a hand that was never invitational — or will “show” a side suit because it is there.

**Objectives (uncontested, matchpoints):**

1. **Stay safe.** The first bid names a destination. We must still be able to stop there.
2. **Play in a major** if we already have eight cards, or it is cheap to look (1-level, Stayman, transfer). Do not look for a second major on a courtesy or constructive raise.
3. **Else play NT.**
4. **Game** only if invitational and accepted, or GF, or **sure**.
5. **Slam later.**

Minors are a tool (stoppers, inverted, 2/1 path), not an objective. We will lie about them at MP.

Judgment is not off. Shape and controls may move you **one bin**. They do not skip quit → game.

| **1NT (11–14)** | **0–10.** Pass, transfer-and-pass, or crawl (4M). **A 10 does not bid 2♠.** | **11–12 no 4M → 2♠ only.** 4M → Stayman then invite. | **13+.** 3NT or game after Stayman/transfer. |
| **1♥ / 1♠** | **0–7.** Pass or 1NT then 2M. | **11–12.** 3♦ (4 trumps) or 1NT then 3M (3 trumps). | **13+.** 2/1 / Jacoby / splinter. **8–10 = 2M**, not 4M. |

## Example — raise with a raise, don’t chase the side major

North `♠ AT753  ♥ Q85  ♦ K87  ♣ 65` — 8 HCP, three hearts, five spades. Dummy ≈ 9. **Constructive raise.**

Jay opens **1♥**.

| | Right | Rainbow |
|--|-------|---------|
| North | **2♥** | 1♠ |
| After 2♦ from South | — | 2♥ sounds like a doubleton; 3♥ / 3NT / 4♥ is a game you never had |

**Why:** Stay safe. The eight-card heart fit is already there. 1♠ is not cheap: we cannot stop in 2♥ honestly. If you already bid 1♠ and they bid 2♦: **2♥** to play. Do not bid 3♥.

**Same hand, majors reversed** (five hearts, three spades, 8 HCP, partner opened **1♠**): **2♠** is right. 2♥ would be 2/1 GF — you already knew not to bid that on 8. 1♠ over 1♥ is *not* GF, but it is still not a raise. The GF brake is not the raise rule. **2M** is the raise rule, both ways.
