# 2NT — 20 to 21

**Law:** SYSTEM.md §2 (2NT opening) and §8  
**Status:** Version 00 · 26_08_27  
**Tell the opponents:** Nothing on the opening. Transfers below are **announced**.

**20–21** balanced. A five-card major is allowed (same as 1NT). We are **not** using Puppet to find it.

**18–19** opens a suit and **jumps 2NT**. **22+** starts **[2♣](Strong-2-Club.md)**.

---

## How high (responder)

Opener is 20 or 21. There is **no invite**. That is the Puppet / 4NT pile — later.

| | You mean | Bid |
|--|----------|-----|
| **Quit** | **0–3** | **Pass.** Or transfer and pass with 5+ of a major. |
| **Game** | **4+** | **3NT**, or Stayman / transfer then game. |

4 opposite 21 is 25. 3 opposite 21 is 24 — pass. Shape and controls can move you **one** step. They do not invent an invite.

Same tree after **2♣ – 2♦ – 2NT** (opener is 22–24). Pass that 2NT only with a bust — see [Strong-2-Club](Strong-2-Club.md).

---

## Responses

Stayman and transfers. That is the whole tree.

| Response | Meaning | Table |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **3♣** | Stayman — you have a **4-card major** and enough for game | Card |
| <span style="color:#1565C0;font-weight:700">3♦</span> | Transfer to hearts | **Announce** |
| <span style="color:#1565C0;font-weight:700">3♥</span> | Transfer to spades | **Announce** |
| **3NT** | To play. No 4-card major to look for. | — |

**Five-card major → transfer**, even if you also have four of the other. Stayman is for four without five.

Opener **completes** the transfer. No super-accepts. Then:

| You bid | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Pass** | Weak. Play 3M. |
| **3NT** | Choice of games (exactly five). Opener can correct with 3+ or a doubleton honour. |
| **4M** | To play (six). |

After **3♣**, opener: **3♦** = no 4-card major; **3♥** = 4 hearts; **3♠** = 4 spades, not 4 hearts. (Both majors: hearts first, same as 1NT.) You then place **3NT** or **4M**.

Do **not** pass 3♦. You bid Stayman with game values, not a bust. A bust with four of a major **passes 2NT**.

## Example — 3-count passes

**Dealer South · neither vul**

South `♠ AKx  ♥ AQx  ♦ KJxx  ♣ Axx` — **20**.  
North `♠ xxx  ♥ Kxx  ♦ xxx  ♣ xxxx` — **3**.

| West | North | East | South |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| | | | 2NT |
| Pass | Pass | Pass | |

**Why:** 20+3 = 23. Quit. 3NT is the leak.

**Tell the opponents:** Nothing.

## Example — transfer, then pass

South `♠ AKx  ♥ AQx  ♦ KJxx  ♣ Axx` — 20.  
North `♠ xx  ♥ Q10xxx  ♦ xx  ♣ xxxx` — 3, five hearts.

| West | North | East | South |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| | | | 2NT |
| Pass | <span style="color:#1565C0;font-weight:700">3♦</span> | Pass | 3♥ |
| Pass | Pass | Pass | |

**Why:** Not enough for 3NT or 4♥. Transfer and pass. Stay safe.

**Tell the opponents:** Announce 3♦ — “transfer to hearts.”

## Example — Stayman, then 4♥

South `♠ AKx  ♥ AQxx  ♦ KJx  ♣ Axx` — 21, four hearts.  
North `♠ Qxx  ♥ Kxxx  ♦ xx  ♣ Qxxx` — 7, four hearts.

| West | North | East | South |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| | | | 2NT |
| Pass | 3♣ | Pass | 3♥ |
| Pass | 4♥ | Pass | Pass |
| Pass | | | |

**Why:** Four-card major, game values → Stayman. Fit → 4♥. If South had bid 3♦ (no major), North would bid 3NT.

**Tell the opponents:** 3♣ is Stayman (on the card).

## Example — 18–19 is not this opening

South `♠ AKx  ♥ AQx  ♦ KJxx  ♣ Kxx` — **19**.

**1♦** (or 1♣), then **jump 2NT** if they bid a suit. Not 2NT. Not 2♣.
