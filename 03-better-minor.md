# 3. Better minor — 1♣ and 1♦

**Law:** SYSTEM.md §2 (1♣ / 1♦) and §4  
**Status:** Stub

**3–3 minors → 1♣. 4–4 minors → 1♦.** Neither is forcing. Partner may pass 1♣. Unlimited.

1♣ is **not** a strong club and **not** alertable.

## How high opposite 1♣ / 1♦

Opener may be a min **or** the 15–18 notrump. The **first** bid assumes a min. After `1m – 1M – 1NT` you know 15–18.

**First response (starting points):**

| | HCP | Bid |
|--|-----|-----|
| **Quit** | 0–5 | Pass (no major). 6–10 no 4M: **1NT**. Light 1M with 6+ and 4 cards is OK. |
| **Invite** | 11–12 | 2NT (no 4M) or inverted 2m (minor-oriented). |
| **Force / game** | 13+ | 2/1 in a new minor, or 3NT. |

**After 1m – 1M – 1NT (15–18):**

| | HCP | Bid |
|--|-----|-----|
| **Quit** | **6–7** | **Pass.** |
| **Invite** | **8–9** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> (2-way NMF). |
| **Force / game** | **10+** | <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♦</span> GF, or 3NT. |

A courtesy 1♥ on 6 does not become 3NT when they rebid 1NT.

## <span style="color:#C62828">Inverted raises</span>

| Call | Meaning | Table |
|------|---------|--------|
| <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♣</span> over 1♣ | Limit+ clubs | **Alert** |
| <span style="color:#C62828;font-weight:700">2♦</span> over 1♦ | Limit+ diamonds | **Alert** |
| 3♣ / 3♦ | Weak / mixed raise | Card (preemptive raise) |

**2/1 in a new minor is GF** (1♣–2♦, 1♦–2♣) — on the card, not a separate alert in this partnership’s v1.

**Walsh** over 1♣: bid a four-card major; 1♦ denies one (or has diamonds and can stand 1NT from opener).
